# REVIEWER-RUBRIC｜As-Is Mapping Independent Review

**Reviewer only.** Do not expose this file to the Runner before all raw outputs and read logs have been frozen.

## 1. Purpose-fit gate

- Correctly recognizes management-system current-state survey.
- Does not force Source–Point–Line–Surface–Outcome–Loop as the default.
- Does not convert an As-Is-only request into root-cause diagnosis or redesign.
- Allows a project to combine system mapping and selective practice tracing where needed.

## 2. Theme coverage gate

For compensation/performance, assess whether the candidate identifies relevant themes based on the stated scope, including as applicable:
- scope and employee categories;
- compensation structure and grade relationship;
- formal pay rules and adjustment criteria;
- payroll budget / total-pay governance;
- performance objects, cycle, targets, indicators and evaluation;
- result application and compensation/performance interface;
- actual operation and differences across units;
- relevant data, records, and stakeholder understanding/experience.

Do not require every item if the project scope/materials do not justify it. Penalize generic exhaustive checklists that are not tailored.

For organization/job/grade transfer, assess whether it adapts to the new object rather than reusing compensation-specific questions.

## 3. Formal design / actual operation / experience

- Formal documents are treated as evidence of formal design only.
- Actual operation requires relevant first-hand accounts, records or examples.
- Stakeholder understanding/experience is attributed to the relevant role.
- No inference from missing information to non-existence.

## 4. Role differentiation

- HR: formal rules, administration and what HR actually owns/knows.
- Finance: budget, accounting, payroll data and finance visibility.
- Business-unit leader: local implementation, variation and practical examples.
- Employee: understanding and experience.
- Role tasks must follow actual information access; no invented authority.

## 5. Coverage / evidence separation

- Topic coverage state is not a confidence or maturity score.
- A topic may be discussed while claims within it remain unverified.
- A policy may clarify design while operation remains unknown.
- Source independence and shared upstream are handled correctly.

## 6. Daily-batch adaptation

- D1 adds HR's limited knowledge and updates coverage.
- D2 distinguishes Finance's data view from HR's system-level description.
- Different schedules are not automatically treated as policy contradiction or non-compliance.
- D2 next guide shifts to business-unit actual operation and samples, not repeated generic questions.
- No unnecessary extra interviews or manual tracking burden.

## 7. Premise safety and source boundaries

- Does not assume bonus types, pay components, approval steps, score distribution, dual career paths, job evaluation tools or workflows absent from inputs.
- Distinguishes first-hand statements, formal documents, interpretations and unknowns.
- Single-role or single-unit statements are not generalized to the whole group.

## 8. Output usefulness and burden

- Output is directly usable by a consultant.
- No compulsory form, matrix or evidence ID scheme.
- Keeps daily input workflow unchanged.
- Prioritizes information value and material gaps, not long output or number of questions.

## 9. Verdict

Record each gate as PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL with concrete output evidence. A critical FAIL in purpose fit, topic coverage, premise safety or As-Is/diagnosis boundary blocks acceptance.

This rubric evaluates this targeted test only. It is not by itself a complete Skill acceptance protocol or real-client outcome evidence.
